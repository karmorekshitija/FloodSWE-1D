# Methodology and Claim Boundaries

## Validated numerical benchmark

The defensible benchmark in this repository is the one-dimensional wet-bed Stoker dam-break problem. The code compares the complete numerical depth profile against the analytical solution, estimates shock speed from the numerical gradient, checks conservation and CFL stability, and repeats the profile comparison on 125, 250, and 500 cells.

## Synthetic exercises

The Manning calibration uses depth observations generated by the same GVF model with fixed perturbations. It demonstrates inverse-calibration mechanics but does not establish field calibration accuracy.

The rainfall-runoff and detention calculation uses an invented hyetograph, a synthetic catchment response, and a 1.35 rainfall multiplier selected for stress testing. It is not an IPCC projection, a historical event reconstruction, or an LSTM reproduction. NSE and KGE here measure agreement between two related synthetic models only.

## Appropriate claims

- Implemented and grid-tested a first-order Rusanov finite-volume solver against the analytical Stoker wet-bed dam-break solution.
- Measured numerical shock propagation from the computed profile and reported the error.
- Demonstrated synthetic Manning-roughness inversion and detention-basin routing.

## Claims not supported by this repository

- Validation on observed flood or rainfall data.
- Reproduction of Kratzert et al. (2018) or any LSTM model.
- An IPCC SSP5-8.5 regional rainfall projection.
- Real-world detention performance or design adequacy.
